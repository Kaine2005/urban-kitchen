The Renovation

From a boarded-up bar
to somewhere worth going.

Every wall, every fitting, every late night. This is the story of how Urban Kitchen came to be, and it's still being written.

When Stacy walked into a run-down, abandoned bar on Lochlands Street, most people saw a lost cause. She saw the boutique restaurant Arbroath had been waiting for.

Where it started

A building with good bones.

The place had been sitting empty and neglected, the kind of spot most walk straight past. But behind the boarded windows and tired fittings was a room with real character, right in the middle of town. It just needed someone willing to put the work in.

Stripped back & rebuilt

Done properly, not quickly.

From a takeaway to a proper sit-down restaurant, bar and beer garden. The same care going into the food is going into every corner of the room. Good ingredients, cooked fresh, done the way it should be.

What's coming

A beer garden to match.

Cold drinks, good food, and somewhere to sit out when the Scottish weather plays nice. The finishing touches are going in now, and the whole journey, from the first day of the build to opening night, is being documented for everyone to follow.

Follow the build

Watch it come together.

Stacy is documenting the whole renovation on social media. Behind-the-scenes, progress updates, and the odd sneak peek of what's coming. Come along for it.
